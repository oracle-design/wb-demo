      View Side

      1. Valve System

        Quick-release, leak-resistant Boston valves. Inflate or deflate in 3-5 minutes.

      2. One Leak Will Not Sink The Ride

        The main tube and both side pontoons inflate separately. If one chamber loses air, the others keep the boat afloat.

      3. Side Pontoons Keep It Upright

        Wide stabilizer pontoons on both sides lower the chance of rolling over in a fast, tight turn.

      4. Grip That Holds In A Hard Turn

        Anti-slip padded EVA seats, with double webbing handles and neoprene knuckle guards to protect your hands.

      5. Pulls Past 4,500 lbs

        Heavy-duty stainless steel tow D-rings on a reinforced tow harness. Pull limit over 4,500 lbs.

      6. Thick Hull That Shrugs Off Sun And Salt

        Heavy-duty reinforced PVC (1100D is the fabric weight) with a UV-resistant coating. Saltwater and mildew resistant.

      7. Seams Are Welded, Not Glued

        Every seam is heat-welded and backed with double-layer reinforcement patches, replacing glue that breaks down in water.
